True tactical / wide

The target. A high, wide view that keeps most or all outfield players visible so you can read shape, pressing, buildup and off-ball movement.

HC

Coach cam

Useful secondary feed focused on managers and benches. Interesting for adjustments and reactions, but not a replacement for a tactical view.

RC

Ref cam

First-person or referee-focused coverage. Great novelty and perspective; poor for studying team structure.

ALT

Alternate broadcast

Only included when it adds useful angles or analysis. Celebrity commentary and other gimmicks are intentionally de-emphasized.
